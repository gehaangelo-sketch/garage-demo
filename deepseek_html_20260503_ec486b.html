Engine Repair

🔍 The Problem:

Engines lose compression, develop leaks, and suffer from worn internals over time — reducing power by up to 30%.

⚠️ Warning Signs:

• Blue or white smoke from exhaust
• Metal knocking or ticking noises
• Check engine light on
• Oil consumption >1L per 1000km
• Loss of power or rough idle

✅ What's Included:
  • Full computer diagnostic scan
  • Compression & leak-down test
  • Oil change with OEM filter
  • Spark plug inspection/replacement
  • Detailed repair report
